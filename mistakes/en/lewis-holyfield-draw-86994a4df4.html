Lewis Holyfield Draw

1999 • Boxing • Referee Mistake • Judging

THE STORY

On March 13, 1999, Lennox Lewis and Evander Holyfield fought to a controversial draw at Madison Square Garden in New York City. Despite Lewis landing 348 punches to Holyfield's 130, judge Eugenia Williams scored the bout 115-113 in favor of Holyfield, while judges Larry O'Connell and Stanley Christodoulou scored it a draw and for Lewis, respectively. The decision led to widespread criticism and prompted investigations into the scoring. (cbsnews.com)

TEAMS / PEOPLE: Lennox Lewis, Evander Holyfield

WHY IT MATTERS

The fight's outcome remains a significant example of disputed boxing decisions, highlighting the impact of judges' scoring on championship bouts and the sport's integrity.
