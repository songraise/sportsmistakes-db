PSG La Remontada Collapse

2017 • Soccer • Team Mistake • Second-Leg Collapse

THE STORY

On March 8, 2017, at Camp Nou in Barcelona, FC Barcelona overcame a 4-0 first-leg deficit by defeating Paris Saint-Germain 6-1 in the second leg of the UEFA Champions League Round of 16. This remarkable comeback, with goals from Luis Suárez, Layvin Kurzawa (own goal), Lionel Messi, Neymar, and Sergi Roberto, resulted in a 6-5 aggregate victory for Barcelona. (fcbarcelona.com)

TEAMS / PEOPLE: PSG, Barcelona

WHY IT MATTERS

This match is widely regarded as one of the greatest comebacks in football history, showcasing Barcelona's resilience and determination.
