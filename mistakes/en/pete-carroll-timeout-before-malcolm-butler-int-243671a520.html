Pete Carroll Timeout Before Malcolm Butler INT

2015 • NFL • Coaching Mistake • Clock Management

THE STORY

In Super Bowl XLIX on February 1, 2015, at University of Phoenix Stadium in Glendale, Arizona, Seattle Seahawks head coach Pete Carroll called a timeout with 26 seconds remaining and the ball on the New England Patriots' 1-yard line. On the ensuing play, quarterback Russell Wilson's pass intended for Ricardo Lockette was intercepted by Patriots' rookie cornerback Malcolm Butler, sealing a 28-24 victory for New England. (seahawks.com)

TEAMS / PEOPLE: Seattle Seahawks, Pete Carroll

WHY IT MATTERS

The timeout and subsequent interception are frequently debated as pivotal moments leading to the Seahawks' Super Bowl loss.
