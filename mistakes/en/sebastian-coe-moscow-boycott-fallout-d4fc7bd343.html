Sebastian Coe Moscow Boycott Fallout

1980 • Track & Field • Organizational Mistake • Olympic Politics

THE STORY

In July 1980, British middle-distance runner Sebastian Coe competed in the Moscow Olympics, despite the UK government's call for a boycott in protest of the Soviet invasion of Afghanistan. Coe secured gold in the 1,500 meters and silver in the 800 meters. His participation highlighted the tension between political directives and athletes' Olympic aspirations.

TEAMS / PEOPLE: Sebastian Coe, Olympic Athletes

WHY IT MATTERS

Coe's decision to compete underscored the complex interplay between politics and sports, reflecting the challenges athletes faced during politically charged times. His achievements in Moscow remain significant in Olympic history.
