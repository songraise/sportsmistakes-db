Brazil Defensive Setup vs Germany

2014 • Soccer • Coaching Mistake • Tactical Decision

THE STORY

On July 8, 2014, at Estádio Mineirão in Belo Horizonte, Brazil, the Brazilian national team faced Germany in the FIFA World Cup semi-final. Brazil's defensive strategy was overwhelmed as they conceded seven goals, resulting in a 7–1 defeat. This match marked Brazil's heaviest World Cup loss and was described as a national humiliation. (en.wikipedia.org)

TEAMS / PEOPLE: Brazil National Team

WHY IT MATTERS

The match is historically notable for its unprecedented scoreline, leading to widespread analysis of Brazil's defensive tactics and their impact on the team's performance.
