Argentina Saudi Arabia Upset

2022 • Soccer • Team Mistake • Upset Loss

THE STORY

On November 22, 2022, at Lusail Iconic Stadium in Qatar, Argentina led 1-0 against Saudi Arabia in their World Cup opener after Lionel Messi's 10th-minute penalty. However, Saudi Arabia's Saleh Al-Shehri and Salem Al-Dawsari scored in the 48th and 53rd minutes, respectively, securing a 2-1 victory. (fifa.com)

TEAMS / PEOPLE: Argentina, Saudi Arabia

WHY IT MATTERS

This unexpected defeat ended Argentina's 36-match unbeaten streak and is considered one of the biggest upsets in World Cup history. (theanalyst.com)
