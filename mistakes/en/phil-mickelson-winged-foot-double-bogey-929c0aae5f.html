THE SPORTSMISTAKES RECORD

Phil Mickelson Winged Foot Double Bogey

2006 • Golf • Player Mistake • Final Hole Decision

THE STORY

In the final round of the 2006 U.S. Open at Winged Foot Golf Club, Phil Mickelson, leading by one stroke, hit his tee shot left into a hospitality tent on the 18th hole. He then struck a tree with his second shot, leading to a double bogey. This misstep allowed Geoff Ogilvy to win the championship by one stroke. (usga.org)

TEAMS / PEOPLE: Phil Mickelson, U.S. Open

WHY IT MATTERS

Mickelson's aggressive play on the final hole, aiming for the green with a driver, resulted in a costly double bogey, costing him the U.S. Open title. (usga.org)
