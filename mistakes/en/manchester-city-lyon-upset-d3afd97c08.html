Manchester City Lyon Upset

2020 • Soccer • Coaching Mistake • Tactical Experiment

THE STORY

On August 15, 2020, Manchester City faced Olympique Lyonnais in the UEFA Champions League quarter-final in Lisbon. City's manager, Pep Guardiola, implemented an unconventional three-man defense, which led to a 3-1 defeat. Lyon advanced to the semi-finals, with Moussa Dembélé scoring twice in the final minutes. (uefa.com)

TEAMS / PEOPLE: Manchester City, Olympique Lyonnais

WHY IT MATTERS

Guardiola's tactical decisions were widely criticized, contributing to Manchester City's continued struggles in advancing beyond the quarter-finals of the Champions League.
