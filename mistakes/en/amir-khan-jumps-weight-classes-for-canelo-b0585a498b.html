Amir Khan Jumps Weight Classes For Canelo

2016 • Boxing • Fighter Mistake • Matchmaking

THE STORY

On May 7, 2016, at the T-Mobile Arena in Las Vegas, Amir Khan moved up two weight classes to challenge Canelo Álvarez for the WBC middleweight title. Despite Khan's speed and agility, Álvarez secured a sixth-round knockout, retaining his title and highlighting the risks of jumping weight divisions in boxing.

TEAMS / PEOPLE: Amir Khan, Canelo Alvarez

WHY IT MATTERS

This bout underscores the challenges and potential pitfalls of fighters moving up multiple weight classes to face champions, emphasizing the importance of weight class considerations in boxing strategy.
