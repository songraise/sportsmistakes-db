United States Grand Prix Tire Gamble

2005 • Formula 1 • Team Mistake • Tire Strategy

THE STORY

During the 2005 United States Grand Prix at Indianapolis, seven teams using Michelin tires withdrew after the formation lap due to safety concerns over tire failures. This left only six cars from three teams using Bridgestone tires to compete. The race proceeded with minimal participation, leading to widespread criticism and fan dissatisfaction.

TEAMS / PEOPLE: Michelin teams, FIA, Formula 1

WHY IT MATTERS

The incident highlighted significant safety and logistical issues within Formula 1, damaging the sport's reputation and leading to changes in tire supplier dynamics.
