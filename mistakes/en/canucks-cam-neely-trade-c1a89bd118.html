Canucks Cam Neely Trade

1994 • NHL • Front Office Mistake • Hockey Mistake

THE STORY

In 1986, the Vancouver Canucks traded forward Cam Neely and a first-round draft pick to the Boston Bruins for Barry Pederson. Neely went on to score 50 goals in a season for Boston, while Pederson's performance in Vancouver was underwhelming. This trade is often cited as a significant misstep in Canucks' history.

TEAMS / PEOPLE: NHL teams and players

WHY IT MATTERS

The trade is frequently discussed as a pivotal error in the Canucks' front office decisions, impacting the team's performance and fan sentiment for years.
