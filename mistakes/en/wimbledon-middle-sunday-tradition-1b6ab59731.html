Wimbledon Middle Sunday Tradition

2010 • Tennis • Tournament Mistake • Scheduling

THE STORY

In 2010, Wimbledon maintained its tradition of a rest day on Middle Sunday, June 27, with no matches scheduled. This practice aimed to allow grass courts to recover and provide a respite for players and spectators. However, the lack of play on Middle Sunday led to scheduling challenges, including a congested 'Manic Monday' with all fourth-round singles matches played on the following day.

TEAMS / PEOPLE: Wimbledon Organizers

WHY IT MATTERS

The Middle Sunday tradition has been a subject of debate due to its impact on tournament scheduling and player fatigue. In 2022, Wimbledon eliminated the Middle Sunday rest day to create a 14-day tournament, addressing these concerns.
