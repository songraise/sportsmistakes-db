Wladimir Klitschko Overlooks Corrie Sanders

2003 • Boxing • Fighter Mistake • Preparation

THE STORY

On March 8, 2003, at Preussag Arena in Hannover, Germany, Wladimir Klitschko defended his WBO Heavyweight Championship against Corrie Sanders. Sanders knocked Klitschko down four times, leading to a second-round technical knockout victory. This upset was named The Ring magazine's Upset of the Year for 2003.

TEAMS / PEOPLE: Wladimir Klitschko, Corrie Sanders

WHY IT MATTERS

This unexpected defeat significantly impacted Klitschko's career trajectory and the heavyweight division's landscape.
