Yankees 2004 ALCS Collapse

2004 • MLB • Team Mistake • Series Collapse

THE STORY

In the 2004 American League Championship Series, the New York Yankees led the Boston Red Sox 3-0. The Red Sox won four consecutive games, clinching the series 4-3. This marked the first time a team overcame a 3-0 deficit in a best-of-seven series. (baseball-reference.com)

TEAMS / PEOPLE: New York Yankees, Boston Red Sox

WHY IT MATTERS

The Yankees' 2004 ALCS collapse remains a significant event in baseball history, highlighting the unpredictability of postseason series and the resilience of the Red Sox.
