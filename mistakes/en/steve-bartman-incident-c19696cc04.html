Steve Bartman incident

2003 • MLB • Fan/Player Mistake • Fan Interference / Missed Catch

THE STORY

On October 14, 2003, during Game 6 of the National League Championship Series at Wrigley Field, Cubs fan Steve Bartman deflected a foul ball away from left fielder Moisés Alou with the Cubs leading 3-0 in the eighth inning. The Marlins then scored eight runs that inning and won 8-3. The Cubs lost Game 7 the next day, eliminating them from the postseason.

TEAMS / PEOPLE: Chicago Cubs; Florida Marlins; Steve Bartman; Moises Alou

WHY IT MATTERS

The incident remains a focal point in discussions about the Cubs' 2003 postseason collapse, highlighting the impact of fan interference on game outcomes.
