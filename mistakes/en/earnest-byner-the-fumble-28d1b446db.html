Earnest Byner The Fumble

1988 • NFL • Player Mistake • Goal-Line Fumble

THE STORY

On January 17, 1988, during the AFC Championship Game at Mile High Stadium, Cleveland Browns running back Earnest Byner fumbled at the Denver Broncos' 2-yard line with 1:05 remaining, preventing a potential game-tying touchdown. The Broncos secured a 38-33 victory, advancing to Super Bowl XXII. (nfl.com)

TEAMS / PEOPLE: Cleveland Browns, Denver Broncos

WHY IT MATTERS

This pivotal moment, known as "The Fumble," is a defining event in NFL history, symbolizing the Browns' near-miss in reaching the Super Bowl.
