PK Subban Traded from Canadiens

2016 • NHL • Front Office Mistake

THE STORY

On June 29, 2016, the Montreal Canadiens traded defenseman P.K. Subban to the Nashville Predators in exchange for defenseman Shea Weber. Subban, 27, was a two-time All-Star known for his offensive prowess, while Weber, 30, was the Predators' captain and a two-time Norris Trophy finalist. The trade was unexpected and left Montreal fans shocked. (si.com)

TEAMS / PEOPLE: Canadiens

WHY IT MATTERS

This trade remains notable due to its impact on both teams' defensive units and the subsequent performances of Subban and Weber in their new environments.
