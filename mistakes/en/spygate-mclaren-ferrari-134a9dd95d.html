Spygate McLaren Ferrari

2007 • F1 • Team Mistake • Spying Scandal

THE STORY

In 2007, McLaren was found in possession of confidential Ferrari technical data, leading to an FIA investigation. The team was fined $100 million and excluded from the Constructors' Championship, though drivers retained their points. The scandal significantly impacted McLaren's reputation and the sport's integrity.

TEAMS / PEOPLE: McLaren, Ferrari

WHY IT MATTERS

The 'Spygate' scandal remains one of the most significant controversies in Formula One history, highlighting the lengths teams may go to gain a competitive edge and leading to stricter regulations within the sport.
