Isiah Kiner-Falefa's Out at Home Plate in 2025 World Series Game 7

2025 • MLB • Player Mistake

THE STORY

In the bottom of the ninth inning of Game 7 of the 2025 World Series, with the Toronto Blue Jays trailing the Los Angeles Dodgers 4-3 and the bases loaded, Daulton Varsho grounded to second baseman Miguel Rojas. Rojas threw home, where catcher Will Smith tagged out pinch-runner Isiah Kiner-Falefa at the plate, ending the game and securing the Dodgers' victory. (mlb.com)

TEAMS / PEOPLE: Toronto Blue Jays, Los Angeles Dodgers, Isiah Kiner-Falefa, Miguel Rojas, Will Smith

WHY IT MATTERS

The play is notable for its impact on the 2025 World Series outcome and has been widely discussed for its strategic implications in high-pressure situations.
