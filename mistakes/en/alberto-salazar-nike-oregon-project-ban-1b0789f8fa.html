Alberto Salazar Nike Oregon Project Ban

2019 • Track & Field • Coaching Mistake • Doping Program

THE STORY

In October 2019, Nike terminated the Oregon Project, its elite distance running program, following coach Alberto Salazar's four-year doping ban. The decision aimed to alleviate the distraction caused by the controversy surrounding Salazar, allowing athletes to focus on their training and competition needs. Nike committed to assisting athletes in transitioning to new coaching setups. Salazar has appealed the ban, which was upheld by the Court of Arbitration for Sport in 2021. (theguardian.com)

TEAMS / PEOPLE: Alberto Salazar

WHY IT MATTERS

The closure of the Oregon Project marked a significant shift in Nike's approach to athlete management and highlighted the impact of doping allegations on sports organizations. The case also brought attention to the broader issue of athlete welfare and the responsibilities of coaches and sponsors in maintaining ethical standards.
