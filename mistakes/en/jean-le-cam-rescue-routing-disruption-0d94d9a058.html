Jean Le Cam Rescue Routing Disruption

2020 • Sailing • Race Management Mistake • Redress Decision

THE STORY

In November 2020, during the Vendée Globe solo sailing race, Kevin Escoffier's boat, PRB, broke in half in the Southern Ocean, forcing him to abandon ship. Fellow competitor Jean Le Cam diverted his course to rescue Escoffier, successfully bringing him aboard after a challenging operation. The International Jury awarded Le Cam a time compensation of 16 hours and 15 minutes for his efforts. (imoca.org)

TEAMS / PEOPLE: Jean Le Cam, Kevin Escoffier, Vendée Globe

WHY IT MATTERS

This incident highlighted the critical importance of seamanship and the willingness of competitors to assist each other in life-threatening situations, underscoring the camaraderie inherent in solo ocean racing.
