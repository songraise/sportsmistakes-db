Overly defensive play creates loss for England FIFA 2026 Semi Final.

2026 • Soccer • Coaching Mistake

THE STORY

In the 2026 FIFA World Cup semi-final on July 15, England led 1-0 against Argentina after Anthony Gordon's 55th-minute goal. Coach Thomas Tuchel made defensive substitutions, including bringing on Ezri Konsa for Gordon, shifting to a back five. Argentina capitalized, equalizing in the 85th minute through Enzo Fernandez and securing a stoppage-time winner with Lautaro Martinez's goal. (fifa.com)

TEAMS / PEOPLE: England Argentina

WHY IT MATTERS

The defensive strategy employed by Tuchel, intended to protect the lead, backfired, leading to England's elimination from the tournament.
