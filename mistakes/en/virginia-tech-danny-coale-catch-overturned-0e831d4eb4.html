Virginia Tech Danny Coale Catch Overturned

2012 • NCAA Football • Referee Mistake • Catch review

THE STORY

In the 2012 Sugar Bowl on January 3, Virginia Tech's Danny Coale made a one-handed, 20-yard touchdown catch in overtime, initially ruled a touchdown. Upon review, officials overturned the call, determining Coale did not have possession before the ball hit the ground. The Hokies then missed a 37-yard field goal attempt, and Michigan won 23–20. (washingtonpost.com)

TEAMS / PEOPLE: Virginia Tech Hokies, Michigan Wolverines, Danny Coale

WHY IT MATTERS

The overturned touchdown remains one of the most disputed catch reviews in major bowl history, highlighting the impact of replay decisions on game outcomes.
