Expos Trade Randy Johnson

1989 • MLB • Front Office Mistake • Trade

THE STORY

On May 25, 1989, the Montreal Expos traded pitchers Randy Johnson, Brian Holman, and Gene Harris to the Seattle Mariners for All-Star pitcher Mark Langston and a player to be named later. This trade was intended to bolster the Expos' pitching staff for a playoff push. (latimes.com)

TEAMS / PEOPLE: Montreal Expos, Seattle Mariners

WHY IT MATTERS

The trade is notable as Randy Johnson developed into a Hall of Fame pitcher, leading to debates about the Expos' decision to trade him.
