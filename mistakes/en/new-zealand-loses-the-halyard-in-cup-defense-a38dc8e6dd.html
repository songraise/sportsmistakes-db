New Zealand Loses the Halyard in Cup Defense

2003 • Sailing • Equipment Mistake • Gear Failure

THE STORY

In the 2003 America's Cup, Team New Zealand faced significant equipment failures during their defense against Alinghi. In the opening race on February 15, 2003, the team withdrew after experiencing multiple gear failures, including a broken boom and flooding in the cockpit. These issues led to a 5-0 series loss to Alinghi, marking the end of New Zealand's eight-year reign as America's Cup holders.

TEAMS / PEOPLE: Team New Zealand, Alinghi

WHY IT MATTERS

This event is notable as it ended Team New Zealand's dominance in the America's Cup, highlighting the critical importance of equipment reliability in high-stakes sailing competitions.
