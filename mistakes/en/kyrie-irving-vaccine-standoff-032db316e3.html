Kyrie Irving Vaccine Standoff

2021 • NBA • Player Mistake • Availability

THE STORY

In October 2021, Brooklyn Nets guard Kyrie Irving refused to comply with New York City's COVID-19 vaccine mandate, which required proof of vaccination for indoor activities. As a result, the Nets announced he would not play or practice until he became a full participant. This decision led to Irving missing 43 regular-season games and two preseason games. He returned to play part-time in December 2021, participating only in away games. (axios.com)

TEAMS / PEOPLE: Kyrie Irving

WHY IT MATTERS

Irving's absence significantly impacted the Nets' performance during the 2021-2022 season, as they faced challenges in maintaining team cohesion and performance without their star player. The situation also sparked broader discussions about individual rights versus public health mandates in professional sports.
