Juan Manuel Marquez Drinks Own Urine Story

2012 • Boxing • Fighter Mistake • Public Relations

THE STORY

In 2009, boxer Juan Manuel Marquez was featured in HBO's '24/7' documentary series, where he discussed his practice of drinking his own urine during training. He believed this ritual, which he had been performing for several fights, contributed positively to his performance. (theguardian.com)

TEAMS / PEOPLE: Juan Manuel Marquez

WHY IT MATTERS

This unconventional practice attracted significant media attention and sparked discussions about athlete training methods.
