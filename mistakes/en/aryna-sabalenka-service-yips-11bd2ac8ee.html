Aryna Sabalenka Service Yips

2022 • Tennis • Player Mistake • Technical Error

THE STORY

In early 2022, Aryna Sabalenka, the world number two tennis player, faced significant serving issues, committing 18 double faults in her first match of the season at the Adelaide International. Despite these challenges, she advanced to the fourth round of the Australian Open, overcoming opponents like Storm Sanders and Markéta Vondroušová, though her serving struggles persisted throughout the tournament. Sabalenka's service woes were a focal point during this period, impacting her performance and raising concerns about her consistency on serve.

TEAMS / PEOPLE: Aryna Sabalenka

WHY IT MATTERS

Sabalenka's service difficulties in 2022 highlighted the mental and technical challenges athletes can face, affecting their performance and rankings. Her ability to address and overcome these issues was crucial for her career trajectory.
