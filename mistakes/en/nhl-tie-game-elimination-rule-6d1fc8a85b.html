NHL Tie Game Elimination Rule

2005 • NHL • League Mistake • Rules

THE STORY

In 2005, the NHL introduced shootouts to resolve tied regular-season games, eliminating ties. This change aimed to ensure a winner in every game, impacting team strategies and standings. The shootout format has been a subject of debate among fans and analysts.

TEAMS / PEOPLE: NHL

WHY IT MATTERS

The rule change significantly altered game outcomes and team standings, leading to ongoing discussions about its impact on the sport.
