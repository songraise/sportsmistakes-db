Jordan Brazil Pit Timing Error

2003 • Formula 1 • Team Mistake • Pit Strategy

THE STORY

At the 2003 Brazilian Grand Prix on April 6, Giancarlo Fisichella of Jordan Grand Prix executed a bold strategy by pitting early under the safety car, refueling to reach 75% race distance without another stop. This gamble positioned him to lead when the race was red-flagged after 54 laps due to accidents. Initially, Kimi Räikkönen was declared the winner, but after an FIA investigation, Fisichella was confirmed as the victor, marking his first Formula One win and Jordan's last. (grandprix.com)

TEAMS / PEOPLE: Jordan Grand Prix

WHY IT MATTERS

This event is notable for the strategic gamble that led to Fisichella's first win and Jordan's final victory in Formula One.
