Sydney Hobart 2024 Man Overboard Rescue

2024 • Sailing • Team Mistake • Safety Failure

THE STORY

On December 27, 2024, during the Sydney to Hobart Yacht Race, Porco Rosso crew member Luke Watkins was swept overboard approximately 60 nautical miles off the New South Wales coast. After about 45 minutes in the water, he was rescued by his crew. (abc.net.au)

TEAMS / PEOPLE: Sydney Hobart fleet

WHY IT MATTERS

This incident underscores the critical importance of crew safety measures, such as tethering and personal locator beacons, in preventing man overboard situations during offshore races.
