Rambler 88 Fastnet Damage Gamble

2019 • Sailing • Equipment Mistake • Structural Damage

THE STORY

In the 2019 Rolex Fastnet Race, Rambler 88, skippered by George David, set a new monohull record to the Fastnet Rock, passing it in 1 day, 2 hours, 47 minutes, 47 seconds, 88 minutes faster than their previous record. The team finished first in monohull line honours but did not break the overall race record, finishing 1 hour and 16 minutes behind the 2011 monohull race record. This performance highlighted the yacht's speed and the crew's skill in challenging conditions.

TEAMS / PEOPLE: Rambler 88, Fastnet Race

WHY IT MATTERS

Rambler 88's 2019 performance demonstrated the yacht's capabilities and the crew's expertise, contributing to the ongoing legacy of the Rambler series in offshore racing.
