Suns Game 7 Collapse vs Mavericks

2022 • NBA • Player Mistake • Basketball Decision

THE STORY

On May 15, 2022, the Phoenix Suns faced the Dallas Mavericks in Game 7 of the Western Conference Semifinals at Footprint Center in Phoenix. The Suns suffered a decisive 123-90 defeat, resulting in their elimination from the playoffs. This loss concluded a season where the Suns had achieved a league-best 64-18 record and advanced to the NBA Finals the previous year.

TEAMS / PEOPLE: NBA teams and players

WHY IT MATTERS

The Suns' unexpected Game 7 loss to the Mavericks marked a significant postseason upset, ending their promising season and raising questions about their playoff performance under pressure.
