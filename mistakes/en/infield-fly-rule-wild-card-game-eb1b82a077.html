Infield Fly Rule Wild Card Game

2012 • MLB • Referee Mistake • Rule Interpretation

THE STORY

In the 2012 National League Wild Card Game between the Atlanta Braves and the St. Louis Cardinals, an infield fly rule was called on a pop-up hit by Andrelton Simmons in the eighth inning. The call was made by umpire Sam Holbrook, leading to confusion and protests from Braves players and fans. The Cardinals won the game 6-3, advancing to the National League Division Series.

TEAMS / PEOPLE: Atlanta Braves, St. Louis Cardinals

WHY IT MATTERS

The controversial infield fly rule call remains a significant moment in MLB history, highlighting the complexities of the rule and its impact on postseason games. It sparked widespread debate and led to discussions about the clarity and application of the infield fly rule.
