Advertising & Partnerships

Reach sports fans when they're paying attention.

SportsMistakes is built around the moments fans can't stop talking about — controversial calls, costly decisions, unforgettable blunders and the debates that can continue for decades.

For advertisers, that creates something valuable: an engaged sports audience actively reading, voting, debating, searching and discovering.

Why SportsMistakes?

A different way to reach sports fans.

01

High-interest sports content

SportsMistakes covers memorable moments across leagues, teams, athletes and eras, giving brands opportunities to appear alongside sports content fans actively choose to explore.

02

More than passive reading

Visitors don't just read. They judge mistakes, vote, explore rankings, debate decisions and discover related moments — creating multiple opportunities for meaningful brand exposure.

03

Evergreen + current

A mistake from last night's game can generate immediate interest. A famous mistake from decades ago can still attract fans years later. SportsMistakes combines timely sports conversation with an expanding archive of searchable sports history.

04

Multiple sports and audiences

The platform spans football, hockey, basketball, baseball, soccer and other sports, with opportunities to align advertising with relevant leagues, categories and content.

05

A distinctive environment

SportsMistakes isn't another scores-and-highlights site. Its focus on decisions, consequences, debate and learning gives advertisers a different way to reach sports fans.

06

Built around discovery

From individual mistakes to rankings, collections and community discussion, SportsMistakes is designed to encourage visitors to keep exploring the record rather than viewing a single piece of content and leaving.
